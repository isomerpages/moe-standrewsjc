<h4><strong>Shining a Light on Student Achievement</strong></h4>
<p>This year’s ceremony recognized a total of <strong>122 JC3 students</strong> alongside <strong>53 JC1 and JC2 awardees</strong> across
various academic and developmental categories. Furthermore, <strong>49 Service Awards</strong> and <strong>66 Colours Awards</strong> were
presented to Saints who embodied the college values of <em>WISE &amp; TRUE</em> through
their leadership, character, and contributions to CCA life.</p>
<p>Our Guest-of-Honour, <strong>Dr. Yeo Li Pheow</strong> (Senior Advisor,

<h4><strong>Honouring Our Village: Staff and Partners</strong></h4>
<p>SAJC’s commitment to forming Servant Leaders is made possible through
the unwavering support of our educators and partners. This year, we celebrated <strong>14 staff members</strong> for
their dedicated service, alongside <strong>23 partners</strong>, including
members of our Parent Support Group (PSG), alumni, and long-serving coaches.&nbsp;</p>
<p>Their quiet dedication forms the backbone of the college, nurturing our
Saints in both their academic and non-academic pursuits.</p>
<p>As our awardees step forward into their next chapters, we remain confident
that they will continue to think deeply, act rightly, and serve with love—shining
ever brighter, like the light of dawn.</p>
