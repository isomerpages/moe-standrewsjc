<p>At SAJC, we believe that nurturing exemplary character goes hand-in-hand
with fostering mental resilience and emotional wellbeing. We are incredibly
proud to look back on last week’s successful completion of our <strong>Mental Health and Wellbeing Week</strong>,
a Student Development Project centered on student wellness and personal
mastery. 🌟</p>
<p></p>
<p>Grounded in the theme <strong>"I got me, I got you, We got us,"</strong> this
week served as a beautiful reminder of the importance of mental health
and the strength of a unified, supportive culture.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Our students engaged deeply with a variety of meaningful activities designed
to build lasting self-care habits and community ties:</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p><strong>Strengthening Connections:</strong> Through the Class Unity Showcase
and student-led wellbeing booths, Saints found creative ways to support
and encourage their peers.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Building Help-Seeking Efficacy:</strong> Our Peer Support Leaders
(PSLs) facilitated critical sessions for the JC1 cohort, equipping them
with the understanding and confidence to seek help when navigating challenges.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Celebrating Resilience:</strong> We rounded off the week beautifully
with a campus-wide Mental Health Roadshow and a high-energy Cohort Run
at the track.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p></p>
<p>🤍 <strong>Honouring the Hearts Behind the Effort:</strong> Bringing an
initiative of this scale to life requires immense collaboration. We want
to acknowledge the efforts put in by our <strong>Peer Support Leaders (PSLs), Saints Advocates, and Mentoring Club </strong>for
leading with outstanding servant leadership.</p>
<p>
<br>Our heartfelt thanks also go out to our dedicated teachers, college staff,
and external partners. We are especially grateful to <strong>Good News Community Services</strong> for
supporting and facilitating our JC2 assembly and Mental Health Roadshow.
Their collaboration helped create a safe, engaging space for our students
to learn and connect. Special thanks to Metropolitan YMCA, Singapore Association
for Mental Health (SAMH), SHINE Children and Youth Services, and REACH
Community Services for making our Mental Health Roadshow a meaningful experience
for our Saints!</p>
<p></p>
<p>By learning to manage our own wellbeing and actively looking out for those
around us, we continue to build a college where every Saint belongs, grows,
and thrives.</p>
<p>
<br>Thank you, Saints, for keeping our family unbroken!</p>
