<h1><strong>Connecting Cultures, Inspiring Hearts</strong></h1>
<p></p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/Announcements/Mother Tongue Fortnight 2026/Picture1.jpg">
</div>
<p><strong>Mother Tongue Fortnight 2026 </strong>was a vibrant celebration
of culture, creativity, and connection, inspiring students to appreciate
the beauty and relevance of their Mother Tongue languages through meaningful
and engaging experiences. Through activities such as Chinese knot tying,
calligraphy, rangoli art, henna drawing, and traditional games, students
explored diverse cultural traditions while gaining a deeper appreciation
of Singapore’s rich multicultural heritage.</p>
<p>The fortnight featured a wide range of interactive and collaborative programmes,
including the Mother Tongue Symposium, Master Programme, Mystery Games,
and Youth Bilingual Discourse.</p>
<p></p>

<p>These platforms encouraged students to communicate confidently, solve
problems collaboratively, and build new friendships using their Mother
Tongue languages. Music sessions featuring nostalgic Xinyao songs further
strengthened students’ connection to local Chinese culture and heritage
through storytelling and song.</p>
<p></p>

<p>More than a cultural celebration, this year's celebrations fostered creativity,
strengthened school spirit, and reaffirmed the enduring value of bilingualism.
Ethnic showcases, investigative challenges, traditional games, and learning
journeys provided immersive opportunities for students to experience their
Mother Tongue languages in authentic and meaningful contexts. Through moments
of play, creativity, and cultural expression, learning became both memorable
and impactful.</p>
<p>Students shared that the programme deepened their understanding of Mother
Tongue cultures and increased their confidence and interest in using the
language in their daily lives. Beyond language learning, Mother Tongue
Fortnight 2026 nurtured curiosity, cultural awareness, and a stronger sense
of identity among students.</p>
