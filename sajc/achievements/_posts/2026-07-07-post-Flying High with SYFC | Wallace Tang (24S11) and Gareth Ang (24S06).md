<p>We are proud to share that Wallace Tang (24S11) and Gareth Ang (24S06)
graduated from the Singapore Youth Flying Club's Private Pilot Licence
course on 4th July 2026.</p>
<p>Aside from receiving his certificate, Gareth also received SYFC's Best-in-Flying
Award at this year's Private Pilot Licence Wings &amp; Aviation Awards
Ceremony.</p>
<p>Congratulations to both young pilots! Up and On!</p>
